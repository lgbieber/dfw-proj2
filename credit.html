dillon ker

Dillon is a San Francisco-based artist and a student at the University of San Francisco. He specializes in all kinds of publication design, from small-scale zines to full magazines, and even coffee-table art books. Dillon uses his SF background and skills in book design and comic-style sketching to create publications with heartfelt stories that often resonate with specific local communities. Many call Dillon Mr. Cool Guy, and his unofficial catchphrase reflects that: “gotta be chill to be a chiller.”

irene duplantier

I have drawn and painted my whole life, but recently have merged my creative abilities with my love for sewing. What began as exact replications of clothes I love has turned into the ability to create an entire pair of pants, adding practical and aesthetic details as I go. Sewing feels like a hack to consumerism, I can take used sheets, tablecloths, or old clothes and transform them into clothes that I am tempted to buy to refrain from doing so because of the unethical repercussions of overconsumption. Sewing my own clothes forces me to be more conscious about my spending habits and brings a lot more satisfaction with my wardrobe.

cole fiegener

Hey! My name is Cole Fiegener, and I am a Design Student-Athlete at the University of San Francisco.

I love looking at and feeling the design choices of the everyday objects that are around me, I love creating graphic design work, working on interior design, and creating content for my design focused instagram. Overall I find that design is a way of interacting with the world and I choose to design the world around me.
