aBouT THe PRoJeCT

This is a student project, meant to highlight other student projects. It was made in response to the question, “What if retail sites could be about more than just a product?” To tackle this question, the site focuses not only on the objects but on their creators as well, acknowledging their experience and skill. While this site is a mock-up of a hypothetical retail store, it may function more effectively as a marketplace wherein artists can show their work and attract the attention of potential future customers.

This site could never have been made without the agreement and active collaboration of all of the artists featured on the site, along with the creator of the site. Thank you to Dillon, Irene, Cole, and Max for making this possible +++
