upcycled shirt » irene

Irene loves to give old, otherwise forgotten garments new life by means of altering them and turning them into something youthful and functional. In this case, Irene cinched the waist and cropped the sleeves to make an otherwise ordinary and forgettable long-sleeve into something of interest. Thanks to Irene’s eye for fashion and her machine sewing skills, this shirt and many other garments like it continue to be worn.

  • Mossimo Supply Co. collared shirt
  • Blue pinstripes
  • Functional breast pocket

meet irene

I have drawn and painted my whole life, but recently have merged my creative abilities with my love for sewing. What began as exact replications of clothes I love has turned into the ability to create an entire pair of pants, adding practical and aesthetic details as I go.
