cambodia man zine » dillon

This is a comic I made CAMBODIA MAN! It is about the mascot from Golden Mountain Seasoning being evil and trying to steal all the flavor in the world, so Cambodia Man needs to stop him. I sold these at Khmer New Year and I got a lot of love and support. I’m hoping to make more in the future; I already have some funny ideas!

  • Quarter sized
  • Hand-drawn style
  • All-ages comic book

meet dillon

Dillon is a San Francisco-based artist and a student at the University of San Francisco. He specializes in all kinds of publication design, from small-scale zines to full magazines, and even coffee-table art books.
