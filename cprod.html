fruitees t-shirt » cole

Cole takes high-quality blank tees and reinvents them into something playful, expressive, and new. Cole’s SOUR Tees are the result of a bright designer using the resources available to him. These T-shirts are each machine-sewn by Cole, who also screen-prints the lemon chest patch and all other black printed details. Cole also double-hems the bottom hem of each shirt to achieve the perfect cropped effect and add extra weight to the shirts.

  • Hand-sewn
  • Hand-printed
  • Heavy tee
